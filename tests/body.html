Appointment Preparation Sequence

  1. Schedule your consultation via MyChart or our referral line.
  2. Verify your network health insurance:
    1. Commercial and employer-sponsored plans:
      1. In-network primary care physician copay
      2. Specialist consultation pre-authorization
    2. University Health Plans and Medicare Advantage
  3. Complete medical history questionnaires prior to arrival.
  4. Bring your photo identification and insurance cards.
